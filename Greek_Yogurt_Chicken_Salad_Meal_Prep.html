Meal Prep Summary

Greek Yogurt Chicken Salad for 8 Meals

A meal-prep version of Well Plated's Greek Yogurt Chicken Salad scaled for 8 portions, which works well as 4 lunches each for 2 people. The goal is a fast, protein-forward prep with simple assembly, clean portioning, and a 2-hour chill so the flavor comes together before the first meal.

Yield 8 meals
Portion Size ~1 cup
Active Prep ~20 min
Chill Time 2+ hours

Batch Plan

  • Scale the original 6-serving recipe by 4/3 to reach 8 meal-prep portions.
  • Prep the full salad once, then divide it into 8 containers after chilling.
  • Use it for sandwiches, lettuce cups, crackers, or over greens depending on the day.

Source recipe: Well Plated - Greek Yogurt Chicken Salad

Quick Notes

  • Original recipe notes say leftovers keep 3 to 4 days in the fridge.
  • Best flavor comes after a 2-hour or overnight chill.
  • Original nutrition is about 228 calories and 27 g protein per ~1 cup serving as written.

Scaled Ingredient Summary

These amounts reflect the original recipe scaled from roughly 6 cups / 6 servings to 8 portions.

Ingredient Amount For 8 Meals Prep Notes
Cooked chicken breast 4 cups Dice into 1/2-inch pieces. Equivalent to about 1 2/3 lb raw chicken breast.
Seedless red grapes 2 2/3 cups Halve them so the salad stays easy to portion.
Celery About 2 cups diced Usually about 4 medium stalks.
Green onions About 1/3 cup Thin slices work best so the dressing coats evenly.
Almonds 2/3 cup Toast first for better crunch.
Plain Greek yogurt 1 1/3 cups Acts as the creamy base in place of mayo.
Milk 2 2/3 tbsp Whisk into the yogurt to loosen the dressing.
Honey 2 2/3 tsp Balances the tang from the yogurt.
Kosher salt 1 1/3 tsp Taste after mixing and adjust if needed.
Black pepper 2/3 tsp Add more after chilling if the flavor needs a lift.
Fresh dill 2 2/3 tbsp Sprinkle on when serving for the freshest flavor.

Step-by-Step Meal Prep

Follow this prep order to keep the salad crisp, evenly mixed, and easy to split into 8 grab-and-go containers.

  1. Prep the mix-ins 5-8 min

    Dice the cooked chicken, halve the grapes, dice the celery, slice the green onions, and chop the dill. If the almonds are not already toasted, toast them first and let them cool.

  2. Build the salad base 2 min

    Add the chicken, grapes, celery, green onions, and almonds to a large bowl so the solid ingredients are ready before the dressing goes in.

  3. Whisk the dressing 2 min

    In a separate bowl, whisk together the Greek yogurt, milk, honey, kosher salt, and black pepper until smooth and evenly combined.

  4. Combine everything 2 min

    Pour the dressing over the chicken mixture and toss until the salad is evenly coated. Taste and adjust salt or pepper if needed.

  5. Chill before portioning 2 hr+

    Refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or overnight if you are prepping ahead, so the flavor settles and the texture firms up.

  6. Portion into 8 meals 5 min

    Divide the salad into 8 containers at about 1 cup each. Add chopped dill right before serving if you want the freshest herb flavor.

  7. Serve your way daily

    Eat it on whole-grain bread, in lettuce cups, with crackers, or over salad greens depending on how you want each meal to feel.

Good prep flow: If you are making 4 lunches each, this batch fits neatly into a Sunday-to-Wednesday lunch run for two people, which stays inside the source recipe's 3 to 4 day fridge window.

Portioning, Storage, and Serving Notes

These are the most useful notes to keep the prep practical once the batch is made.

Portioning

Plan on about 1 cup per meal. If you want identical containers, portion by weight after mixing so each meal lands close to the same size.

Storage

The source recipe says leftovers keep 3 to 4 days in the refrigerator, so this 8-meal batch works best as a short run of weekday lunches.

Best Flavor

The salad tastes better after a rest. A 2-hour chill is the minimum target, and overnight works if you want it ready first thing the next day.

Nutrition

As written, the original recipe lists each serving at roughly 228 calories with 27 g protein. Extra bread, crackers, or larger portions will change that.

Serving Ideas and Helpful Extras

Rotate the base salad through a few serving formats so the batch does not feel repetitive.

Fastest Lunch

Pack one container of salad plus crackers or lettuce leaves and eat it cold straight from the fridge.

Sandwich Option

Use whole-grain bread for a more filling lunch and keep the bread separate until you are ready to eat.

Greens Bowl

Spoon one portion over salad greens when you want the chicken salad to feel lighter but still filling.

Prep Shortcut

If you already have cooked chicken ready, the only real work is chopping, whisking, mixing, and chilling.
